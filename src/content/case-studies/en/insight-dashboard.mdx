_Insight Dashboard is a module of the healthcare supply chain platform. It lets users view spend reports and analyze purchasing for each hospital organization._

## Problem

Users open the dashboard and filter spend by period, corporation, vendor, then drill down.

## Approach & alternatives considered

Why Elasticsearch here:

1. The access pattern is read-heavy aggregation with several filters and group-bys, which PostgreSQL handled at about 15s for one API.
2. Data is derived from the primary database, so PostgreSQL stays the **single source of truth** and Elasticsearch is a rebuildable read model.
3. Fast, consistent filtering matters more here than transactional guarantees.
